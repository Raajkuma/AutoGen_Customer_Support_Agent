# Product FAQ

## Damaged or defective products
If an item arrives damaged or does not work, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We offer a free replacement or a full refund, your choice. Pickup is free for damaged items.

## Warranty
Electronics (earbuds, smart watches, blenders, lamps) carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Footwear and bags carry a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty claims need the invoice.

## Payments and billing
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. If a payment fails but money is debited, the amount is auto-reversed within 5-7 business days. Duplicate charges are verified by the billing team and refunded within 5-7 business days after verification. Billing disputes need the order ID and transaction reference.

## Order cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before dispatch from My Orders. After dispatch, refuse delivery or use the return process.
