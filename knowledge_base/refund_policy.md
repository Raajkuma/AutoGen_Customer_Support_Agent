# Refund and Return Policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in original packaging, with tags and invoice. Hygiene products such as earbuds with opened seals and yoga mats that were used are not returnable.

## How to return an item
1. Open My Orders and select the delivered order.
2. Choose Report a problem and select Return or refund.
3. A courier pickup is scheduled within 2 business days. No return shipping fee for defective or wrong items; Rs 79 is deducted for change-of-mind returns.

## Refund timeline
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes quality check. Refunds to UPI and wallets can arrive in 2-3 business days.

## Replacements
Defective or wrong items can be replaced instead of refunded. Replacements are shipped within 3 business days of pickup approval.
