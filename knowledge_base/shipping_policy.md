# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities (Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru) and 5-8 business days elsewhere in India. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs Rs 99. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment.

## Tracking your order
Once dispatched, a tracking link is sent by email and SMS. You can also see the order status under My Orders. Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delayed orders
If your order has not arrived 2 business days after the expected delivery date, contact support. We will trace the shipment with the courier within 24 hours. If it is delayed by more than 7 days, you can choose a full refund or a re-shipment at no extra cost.

## Lost or undelivered packages
If tracking shows Delivered but you have not received the package, report it within 48 hours. We open a courier investigation that takes up to 5 business days, after which we send a replacement or refund.

## Shipping charges
Orders above Rs 999 ship free. Below Rs 999, shipping is Rs 49.
