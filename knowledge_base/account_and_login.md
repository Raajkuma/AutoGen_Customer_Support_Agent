# Account and Login

## Reset your password
1. On the login page select Forgot password.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Open the reset link we email you. It is valid for 30 minutes.
4. Choose a new password with at least 8 characters, one number and one symbol.
If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.

## Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts the account locks for 30 minutes. It unlocks automatically; no support request is needed.

## Change email or phone number
Go to Account Settings, select Edit profile, and confirm the change using the one-time code sent to your current email address.

## Delete your account
Account deletion requests are processed within 7 days. Open orders must be completed first.
